Your downloads. Your business.

No account. No analytics.
No ads.

Nothing about you or your files is collected. Choose how you connect, remove tracking tags, and decide whether to keep download history.

  • Proxy and Tor: Hide your IP with Tor or a SOCKS5/HTTP proxy. If the proxy fails, downloads stop instead of exposing your real address.
  • Torrent VPN kill switch: Torrents only run while your VPN is connected.
  • Privacy signals: Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are sent with every request.
  • Cleaner links: Tracking tags such as utm_, fbclid, and gclid are removed from links.
  • Your history, your choice: Clear your download history any time, or don’t keep it at all.

Get started

From download
to desktop.

Follow the steps for your system. FastDownloader is not code-signed yet, and the macOS app is not notarized, so your system may ask for extra confirmation.

Install

  1. Open FastDownloader-Setup-1.3.0.exe and follow the installer.
  2. Find FastDownloader in the tray next to the clock.

If something goes wrong

  • If Windows says “Windows protected your PC,” check that you downloaded the official release and verify its SHA-256 digest. If you decide to proceed, select More info → Run anyway.
  • FastDownloader is not code-signed yet. This warning is not a safety verdict about the file.

Install

  1. Open the .dmg for your Mac’s chip.
  2. Drag FastDownloader into Applications.
  3. Open FastDownloader from Applications.

If something goes wrong

  • If macOS blocks the first launch, check the release source and SHA-256 digest. To proceed, open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
  • If macOS says the app “is damaged,” the documented workaround is to run this in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/FastDownloader.app
  • That command removes the app’s quarantine attribute. Use it only after checking the download and deciding to proceed.

Install

  1. On Ubuntu or Debian, install the .deb with sudo apt install ./FastDownloader-*.deb
  2. Or, for the AppImage, make it executable with chmod +x FastDownloader-*.AppImage
  3. Then open the AppImage.

If something goes wrong

  • If the AppImage says “AppImages require FUSE to run,” run sudo apt install libfuse2 (on Ubuntu 24.04: libfuse2t64).
  • If the AppImage closes straight away or reports a sandbox problem on Ubuntu 24.04 or newer, the documented workaround is ./FastDownloader-*.AppImage --no-sandbox
  • The --no-sandbox option turns off the app sandbox, so try the .deb package first on Ubuntu or Debian.

Please download only what you have the right to. Many video sites’ terms, including YouTube’s, don’t allow downloading, and DRM-protected services aren’t supported.

Questions

A few things
before you download.

Is FastDownloader free?+

Yes. FastDownloader is free to use and licensed under the MIT licence.

Which systems are supported?+

Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit; macOS with Apple Silicon or Intel processors; and Linux through AppImage or an Ubuntu/Debian .deb package.

Which Mac download do I need?+

Open Apple menu → About This Mac. Choose Apple Silicon for an Apple M-series chip, or Intel for an Intel processor.

Can I resume downloads after restarting?+

Yes. FastDownloader supports pausing and resuming downloads, including after closing the app or restarting your computer.

Which browsers work with the extension?+

Extension v1.6.1 is available for Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi, and Firefox. It adds video download controls, right-click link downloads, and ad and tracker blocking.

Can I download any video, including DRM-protected content?+

DRM-protected services are not supported. Download only content you have the right to download. Many video sites’ terms, including YouTube’s, do not allow downloading. Available video quality depends on the source.

What data does FastDownloader collect?+

Nothing about you or your files is collected. There is no account or analytics. You can clear download history whenever you want or choose not to keep it.

Why does my system show an installation warning?+

FastDownloader is not code-signed yet, and the macOS app is not notarized. Check the official release source and published SHA-256 digest before deciding to proceed. See the installation section for your system’s steps.
