04 / Browser extension

Works where
you browse.

One extension for every major browser. It adds a Download button to videos with a quality menu, sends links to FastDownloader with a right-click, and blocks well-known ad and tracking networks along the way.

For Chrome For Firefox
Chrome version also works in Edge Brave Opera Vivaldi
  1. Unzip the extension into a folder you keep.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (or edge://, brave://, opera://) and switch on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked, choose the folder, then pin it from the puzzle-piece menu.
  4. Shortcut: in FastDownloader click the puzzle icon → Add to … and it does steps 1–2 for you.
05 / Privacy

No account.
No analytics.
No ads.

FastDownloader only talks to the sites you download from. Nothing about you or your files is collected.

  • Hide your IP with Tor or any SOCKS5/HTTP proxy. If the proxy fails, downloads stop instead of leaking your real address.
  • VPN kill switch for torrents: they only run while your VPN is connected.
  • Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control sent with every request.
  • Tracking tags removed from links (utm_, fbclid, gclid…).
  • History your way: clear it any time, or don't keep it at all.
06 / Install
Windows
  1. Run FastDownloader-Setup-1.0.0.exe.
  2. If you see "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway. The app isn't code-signed yet; this isn't a virus warning.
  3. FastDownloader lives in the tray next to the clock.
macOS
  1. Open the .dmg and drag FastDownloader into Applications.
  2. First launch: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
  3. If macOS says the app "is damaged", run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/FastDownloader.app in Terminal.
Linux
  1. AppImage: chmod +x FastDownloader-*.AppImage, then open it.
  2. Ubuntu / Debian: sudo apt install ./FastDownloader-*.deb
Android
  1. Download the APK on your phone and open it.
  2. The first time, Android asks to allow installs from your browser: tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then Install.
  3. To download a link from any app, tap Share and pick FastDownloader.

Please download only what you have the right to. Many video sites' terms (YouTube's included) don't allow downloading, and DRM-protected services aren't supported.
